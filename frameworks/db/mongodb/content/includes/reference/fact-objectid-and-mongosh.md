Starting in MongoDB 5.0, :binary:`mongosh` replaces the legacy `mongo` shell. The `ObjectId()` methods work differently in `mongosh` than in the legacy `mongo` shell. For more information on the legacy methods, see `Legacy mongo Shell <mongo>`.
